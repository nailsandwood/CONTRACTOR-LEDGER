Backup & Files

Data is automatically saved to your Google Drive. You can also download a local copy or restore from a file.

Google Drive Status

Your ledger is stored as contractor-ledger-data.json inside the folder Contractor Ledger in your Google Drive.

Save Backup (Computer or Drive)

Download a restorable JSON file to your computer, or upload it straight into your Google Drive folder.

Restore from Computer File

Choose a backup file to restore. This replaces all current data and uploads it to Drive.


Unmark All Dead

Unmarks every contractor currently tagged "Dead" — nothing else changes.

Close Session — Clear All Entries

Empties every contractor's ledger at once. Contractor info stays. A safety backup is saved automatically first.

Clear Contractor Summary

Removes every contractor and their account. This cannot be undone, so save a backup first.
